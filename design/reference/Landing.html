How it works

1 · बोलिए

Press the mic and say it the way you would to your munshi: size, grade, quantity, site, deadline, credit. Hindi, English or both.

2 · मोलभाव

Molbhav calls nearby dealers at once, gets rate, GST, delivery, unloading and credit, then calls the best two back with the best real total.

3 · पक्का

You get one parchi: cheapest landed price, delivery time, credit days and every recording. Say “confirm” and it is done.

Honest bargaining, always

Dealers keep picking up only if we are straight with them. These rules are built into the product, not just promised.

  • Every AI call says it is an AI and is recorded, in the first sentence.
  • Only real quotes are ever cited, never another dealer’s name, never a made-up number.
  • Your regular dealer is always included and gets a fair chance to match.
  • No dealer can pay to rank higher. We work for the buyer.

For dealers: असली ऑर्डर, असली quantity

Every Molbhav call is a real buyer with a real order and a real site. Short calls, in Hindi, during working hours, never on Sunday.
